# Paper Presentation — Instructions

> **Chosen paper:** *H2-LLM: Hardware-Dataflow Co-Exploration for Heterogeneous Hybrid-Bonding-based Low-Batch LLM Inference* (ISCA 2025, Best Paper). Notes: [ARTICLE_H2-LLM.md](ARTICLE_H2-LLM.md)

## Task
Pick **one** recent chip or hardware-accelerator architecture paper. Make your own slides, in your own words, that explain its motivation, architecture, key features, and experimental results.

## Format (5 minutes total, including Q&A)

| # | Slide | What it must answer |
|---|-------|---------------------|
| 1 | **Motivation** (1 slide) | What problem does the work address, and why does it matter? |
| 2 | **Architecture Overview** (1 slide) | What is the proposed architecture, and how does it work? |
| 3–4 | **Key Features** (1–2 slides) | What are the key innovations or unique design features? |
| 4–5 | **Experimental Results** (1–2 slides) | What are the most important results, and what do they show? |
| last | **Your Thoughts** | Think like a chip architect. Pose **two questions of your own** about the paper's design choices, what surprised you, or what you'd try next. Give your answer or reflection for each. |

Total: about 5–7 slides. At about 5 minutes, plan for roughly 45–60 seconds per slide.

## Paper Selection Rules
- Published **2024–2026**.
- From a **leading ACM/IEEE conference or journal**, including but not limited to:
  - Conferences: DAC, ICCAD, FCCM, FPGA, HPCA, ISCA, MICRO, MLSys, Hot Chips, etc.
  - Journals: IEEE TCAD, IEEE TC, ACM TODAES, ACM TRETS, etc.
- You **may NOT** choose a paper written by the instructor or by yourself.

## Submission
- The slides.
- The **2 questions and answers** (short answers) about your presentation.
